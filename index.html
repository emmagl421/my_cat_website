Backstory

I found Nugget when he was about a month and a half old at a local Mcdonalds. He was all by himself and I waited a couple weeks to make sure there were no other cats before I took him. I caught him with some tuna and hope.

Now Nugget is 5 years old and happily living with me. He likes to watch the birds and other creatures out the window and eat. He is a large cat. He loves to get attention and pets. He uses all 2 of his brain cells to cause problems and be silly.
